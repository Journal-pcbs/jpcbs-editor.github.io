# Publication ethics and peer review
JPCBS follows the principles of the Committee on Publication Ethics ([COPE](https://publicationethics.org/core-practices)) and the ICMJE recommendations.

## Peer review
Each manuscript is assessed by the editor, then by at least two independent experts. Reviewers do not review work where they have a conflict of interest and treat manuscripts as confidential. The editor decides: accept, minor revision, major revision or reject. Decisions rest on scientific merit, not on the authors' identity or origin.

## Authorship
Authorship requires a substantial contribution, approval of the final text and accountability for the work. Gift or ghost authorship is not accepted.

## Plagiarism and data integrity
Submissions are checked for textual overlap. Fabrication, falsification, image manipulation and duplicate publication are treated as misconduct.

## Competing interests and funding
Authors, reviewers and editors must declare financial or personal interests that could influence the work.

## Corrections, retractions and complaints
Errors are corrected through a dated correction notice. Articles are retracted only for serious problems, with the original kept online and clearly marked. Send concerns to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}); they are handled confidentially and answered in writing.

## Use of AI tools
Authors must disclose any use of generative AI in preparing a manuscript. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and authors remain responsible for all content.
