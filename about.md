# About the Journal

## Journal of Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Biosciences (JPCBS)

The **Journal of Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Biosciences (JPCBS)** is an independent scholarly publishing initiative dedicated to advancing research and scientific knowledge in pharmaceutical chemistry, drug discovery, pharmaceutical sciences, biochemistry, and natural bioactive compounds.

JPCBS aims to provide an academic platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, and students to communicate original scientific findings, exchange innovative ideas, and contribute to developments in pharmaceutical and biomedical research.

## Aim and Objectives

The primary aim of JPCBS is to promote high-quality scientific research and encourage interdisciplinary collaboration across pharmaceutical and bioscience disciplines.

The journal seeks to:

- Encourage original and innovative research in pharmaceutical chemistry and related sciences.
- Promote research in drug discovery, synthesis, characterization, and biological evaluation of bioactive compounds.
- Support interdisciplinary studies connecting pharmaceutical sciences, chemistry, and biosciences.
- Provide a platform for emerging researchers and established scientists.
- Encourage scientific integrity, transparency, and responsible research practices.

## Scope of the Journal

JPCBS welcomes scholarly contributions in, but not limited to, the following areas:

1. Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Medicinal Chemistry
2. Drug Design and Drug Discovery
3. Pharmaceutical Analysis and Quality Control
4. Synthetic and Natural Product Chemistry
5. Marine Natural Products and Bioactive Compounds
6. Pharmacology and Biological Evaluation
7. Biochemistry and Molecular Biosciences
8. Pharmaceutical Formulation and Drug Delivery
9. Polymer Chemistry and Pharmaceutical Applications
10. Computational Chemistry and Molecular Modeling

## Types of Manuscripts

The journal intends to consider the following manuscript categories:

- Original Research Articles
- Review Articles
- Short Communications
- Research Communications

All submissions will be subject to editorial assessment and the journal's applicable peer-review and publication policies.

## Publication Philosophy

JPCBS is committed to encouraging responsible scientific communication, academic integrity, transparency, and ethical publishing practices.

The journal aims to develop a credible scholarly platform that supports the dissemination of meaningful research and contributes to the advancement of pharmaceutical and bioscience research at national and international levels.
