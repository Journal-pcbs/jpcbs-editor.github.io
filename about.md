# About the journal
The Journal of Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Biosciences (JPCBS) is an independent, peer-reviewed scholarly journal. It provides a place to publish and discuss research across pharmaceutical sciences, chemistry and the biosciences.

## Open access and licensing
Articles are free to read and download. Unless stated otherwise on an article, they are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0), and authors keep their copyright.

## Archiving and permanence
Every published article receives a permanent URL and a downloadable PDF. Articles are never removed once published; corrections are issued as dated notices linked from the original article (see [Publication Ethics]({{ '/publication-ethics/' | relative_url }})).

## Publication frequency
Articles are published online as they are accepted and collected into volumes and issues in the [archive]({{ '/archive/' | relative_url }}).
