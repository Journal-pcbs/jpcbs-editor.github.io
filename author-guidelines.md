# Author guidelines

## Before you submit
- The work must be original and not under consideration elsewhere.
- All authors must have approved the submitted version.
- Declare funding, competing interests and, where relevant, ethics approval.

## How to submit
Email your manuscript as one Word (.docx) file, with a separate cover letter, to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Use the subject line "Submission: short title".

## Manuscript structure
1. Title, authors, affiliations and corresponding author (with ORCID where available)
2. Abstract (up to 250 words) and 4 to 6 keywords
3. Introduction
4. Materials and methods, with enough detail to reproduce the work
5. Results and discussion
6. Conclusions
7. Declarations: funding, conflicts of interest, ethics, data availability, author contributions
8. References, numbered and in one consistent style, with DOIs where they exist

## Chemistry and biology reporting
- New compounds: give names, characterisation data (such as NMR, MS, and purity) and synthetic procedures.
- Biological work: state the assays, controls, statistics and replicates; follow ARRIVE guidelines for animal studies and the Declaration of Helsinki for human studies.
- Computational work: report software, versions, parameters and validation.

## Review process
Editors screen each submission for scope, originality and similarity (plagiarism check). Suitable papers go to at least two independent reviewers. See [Publication Ethics]({{ '/publication-ethics/' | relative_url }}).
