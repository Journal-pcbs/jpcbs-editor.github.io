
# Journal of Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Biosciences (JPCBS)

The Journal of Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Biosciences (JPCBS) is an independent scholarly publishing initiative focused on interdisciplinary research in pharmaceutical sciences, pharmaceutical chemistry, and biosciences.

The journal's research interests include medicinal chemistry, drug discovery and development, pharmaceutical analysis, drug delivery systems, pharmacology, biotechnology, biochemistry, and natural products, including marine-derived bioactive compounds.

JPCBS aims to promote scientific research involving the synthesis, characterization, computational investigation, biological evaluation, and pharmaceutical applications of bioactive compounds.

The journal is being developed with an emphasis on academic integrity, transparent editorial practices, ethical research, and open access to scientific knowledge.
